# Nimble Name

## Your files, finally named like you meant to.

`Scan_0042.pdf`. `IMG_7731.jpg`. `document(3).pdf`. Sound familiar?

Nimble Name reads your PDFs and photos, works out what they are, and gives them names you'll actually recognise. It runs on your Mac, and your documents stay on your Mac.

### How it works

1. **Drop.** Drag in files or whole folders.
2. **Look.** Nimble Name reads each document and suggests a name: date, sender, what it is, and the amount.
3. **Rename.** Check the list, tweak anything you like, click once. Done.

Nothing gets renamed until you say so, and if you change your mind, one click undoes the whole batch.

---

### Why you'll like it

- **Private by design.** Nimble Name uses an AI model running on your own Mac. Your bank statements never leave the building.
- **Set it and forget it.** Point it at your Downloads or scanner folder. New files get analysed in the background, and a notification lets you know when they're ready.
- **Your names, your rules.** Change the naming pattern, edit any suggestion, or add a reference number straight from the full-size preview.
- **Handles the messy stuff.** Invoices, receipts, contracts, letters, even handwritten notes and phone photos.
- **Quietly helpful.** A small menu bar icon shows how many files are waiting for you.
- **Feels like a Mac app.** Because it is one: native, fast, keyboard-friendly, with Quick Look-style previews.

---

### What you need

- A Mac with Apple Silicon running macOS 26 or later
- A free local AI server such as oMLX, LM Studio or Ollama (any OpenAI-compatible service works too)
- Supported files: PDF, JPG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF and WebP
